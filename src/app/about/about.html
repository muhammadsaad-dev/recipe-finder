About the Project

Crafted for Foodies, Home Cooks & Creators

RecipeFinder is a modern culinary application designed to make discovering, preparing, and organizing delightful home-cooked meals effortless and enjoyable.

🔍

Smart Recipe Search

Explore a vast catalog of over 300,000 recipes. Search by ingredient, meal name, or dish type with instant responsive suggestions.

📋

Interactive Prep Checklist

Keep track of your kitchen workflow with interactive ingredient checklists you can tick off while prepping your ingredients.

❤️

Persistent Favorites

Save recipes directly to your personal cookbook with one click, securely persisted to your device for instant offline access.

🥗

Nutritional & Dietary Badges

Instantly identify gluten-free, vegan, vegetarian, and healthy choices alongside cook times and serving estimates.

Architecture & Tech Stack

Engineered with modern frontend standards and clean component architecture.
